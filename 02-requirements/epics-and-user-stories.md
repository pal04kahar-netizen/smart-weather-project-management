# Requirements

## Project Epics

The Smart Weather Platform was organized into five major epics in Jira.

### EPIC 1 – Client Registration

Focus: Enable users to register and access the weather platform.

Key requirements:

- Enable registration for free and paid users
- Create a sign-up page
- Create a login page
- Implement validation
- Provide forgot-password functionality

### EPIC 2 – Business Logic

Focus: Build the business logic required to collect, reconcile, and store weather data.

Key requirements:

- Integrate with public weather services
- Reconcile and aggregate weather data from multiple service providers
- Create a database/schema for storing weather data based on location

### EPIC 3 – Building Products and Services

Focus: Provide weather-related products and services across the web and mobile platforms.

Key requirements:

- Detect user location using GPS or IP
- Provide severe weather advisories
- Display current weather information
- Provide 5, 10, and 15-day forecasts
- Provide satellite imagery
- Create a responsive portal design
- Publish APIs/services for client applications
- Create iOS and Android applications

### EPIC 4 – Business Model

Focus: Define business capabilities that support monetization of the platform.

Key requirements:

- Provide access control for paid services
- Provide advertising capabilities on the portal and applications

### EPIC 5 – Business Innovation

Focus: Support future business innovation initiatives for the weather platform.

## Requirement Management Approach

Requirements were translated into Jira epics, stories, and tasks. Story points were assigned to support relative estimation and sprint planning.

The requirements were then organized in the product backlog and selected for sprint execution based on priority and project goals.
