# Smart Weather Platform – Sprint Reports

## Sprint Reporting Overview

Sprint progress was monitored using Jira Scrum boards and Agile reporting practices. The project was planned across three sprints, with each sprint having a defined focus and set of planned work items.

Sprint reporting was used to review progress, identify remaining work, and evaluate sprint outcomes.

---

## Sprint 1 – Client Registration & Business Logic

### Sprint Goal

Establish the initial foundation of the Smart Weather Platform by addressing client registration and core business logic.

### Planned Work

- Client registration requirements
- User-related functionality
- Business rules and validation
- Initial product foundation
- Supporting tasks for planned user stories

### Progress Tracking

Sprint work was tracked through the Jira Scrum board using the workflow:

**To Do → In Progress → Done**

Progress was reviewed throughout the sprint to identify completed, active, and pending work.

### Sprint Outcome

Sprint 1 established the initial product foundation and prepared the project for subsequent weather platform functionality.

---

## Sprint 2 – Product/Service Development

### Sprint Goal

Develop the core product and service capabilities of the Smart Weather Platform.

### Planned Work

- Weather information functionality
- Weather forecast functionality
- Product/service development
- Customer-focused requirements
- Supporting implementation tasks

### Progress Tracking

User stories and tasks were monitored through the Scrum board. Work status was updated as activities progressed through the sprint.

### Sprint Outcome

Sprint 2 focused on expanding the platform beyond the initial foundation and delivering core product and service capabilities.

---

## Sprint 3 – Business Innovation

### Sprint Goal

Focus on business innovation, product improvements, and future enhancement opportunities.

### Planned Work

- Business innovation initiatives
- Product enhancements
- Customer experience improvements
- Future platform opportunities
- Supporting tasks

### Progress Tracking

Sprint progress was monitored through Jira, with work items tracked according to their current status.

### Sprint Outcome

Sprint 3 focused on extending the product vision through innovation and enhancement initiatives.

---

## Burndown and Burnup Tracking

Agile reporting concepts such as burndown and burnup were used to understand sprint progress.

### Burndown

A burndown view helps track the amount of remaining work during a sprint. As work is completed, the remaining work should decrease.

### Burnup

A burnup view helps visualize completed work against the total scope of the sprint or release.

These reports provide visibility into:

- Remaining work
- Completed work
- Sprint progress
- Scope changes
- Overall delivery trend

---

## Sprint Review

At the end of each sprint, the completed work was reviewed against the sprint goal.

The review considered:

- Completed user stories
- Remaining work
- Sprint objectives
- Product progress
- Potential improvements for subsequent sprints

---

## Sprint Retrospective

The retrospective focused on identifying improvements for the next sprint.

Areas considered included:

- What went well
- What could be improved
- Challenges encountered
- Process improvements
- Actions for the next sprint

---

## Overall Project Progress

The three-sprint structure provided an incremental approach to product development:

| Sprint | Primary Focus | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Sprint 1 | Client Registration & Business Logic | Product foundation |
| Sprint 2 | Product/Service Development | Core platform capabilities |
| Sprint 3 | Business Innovation | Enhancements and future opportunities |

The project used Agile/Scrum practices to organize requirements, prioritize work, plan sprints, track execution, and review progress.
