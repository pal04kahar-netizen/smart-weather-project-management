# Smart Weather Platform – Product Backlog

## Backlog Overview

The product backlog contains the prioritized work required to develop the Smart Weather Platform. Requirements were organized into epics and user stories and prioritized based on business value and planned sprint delivery.

## Backlog Structure

The project backlog was organized around five major epics:

1. Client Registration
2. Business Logic
3. Building Products and Services 
4. Business Model
4. Business Innovation 

## Prioritized Backlog

| Priority | Epic | Example User Story | Sprint |
|---|---|---|---|
| High | Client Registration | User can register for an account | Sprint 1 |
| High | Client Registration | User can manage basic profile information | Sprint 1 |

## Backlog Prioritization

Backlog items were prioritized using the following considerations:

- Business value
- Customer/user value
- Product dependencies
- Sprint goals
- Feasibility within the planned sprint

### Sprint 2

### Sprint 3
Focus:

## Agile Backlog Management

The backlog was maintained as a prioritized list of work items. User stories were broken down into smaller tasks and allocated to appropriate sprints for execution.

The backlog was reviewed and managed through Jira during sprint planning and execution.
