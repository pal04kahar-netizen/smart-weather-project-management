# Sprint Planning

## Sprint Planning Approach

The Smart Weather Platform was planned using an Agile/Scrum approach in Jira.

The product backlog was reviewed and work was selected into sprints based on the project goals and planned scope. Each selected issue had a story point estimate, allowing the sprint scope to be measured and tracked.

The project was planned across three sprints as part of the overall release plan.

# Sprint Planning Activities

The sprint planning process involved:

1. Reviewing the product backlog
2. Grouping work by epic
3. Selecting backlog items for the sprint
4. Reviewing story point estimates
5. Defining the sprint goal
6. Assigning issues to the sprint
7. Tracking execution through the Scrum board
8. Monitoring progress using Jira reports
9. Reviewing completed and remaining work

---

# Sprint Tracking

Jira was used throughout sprint execution to provide visibility into:

- Sprint scope
- Issue status
- Story points
- Completed work
- Remaining work
- Sprint progress
- Sprint completion

The sprint board and Agile reports provided a visual way to monitor whether work was progressing toward the sprint goal.
