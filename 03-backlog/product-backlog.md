# Product Backlog

## Backlog Management

The product backlog was maintained in Jira and organized around the five project epics.

The backlog contained requirements represented as user stories and tasks. Each item was assigned a story point estimate to support relative sizing and sprint planning.

The backlog was used as the source for selecting work for the planned sprints.

## Backlog Structure

The backlog was organized into the following epics:

1. Client Registration
2. Business Logic
3. Building Products and Services
4. Business Model
5. Business Innovation

## Story Point Estimation

Story points were used as the estimation field in Jira.

The project used relative sizing rather than estimating work directly in hours.

The backlog items shown in the Jira project were primarily estimated using values such as:

- 1 point
- 2 points
- 3 points

The estimates supported sprint planning and helped visualize the amount of work selected for each sprint.

## Sprint Allocation

Backlog items were selected into sprints based on the project plan.

### Sprint 1

## Backlog Management Activities

The Jira backlog was used to:

- Capture requirements as Jira issues
- Organize issues under epics
- Assign story point estimates
- Select work for sprints
- Track issue status
- Monitor completed and remaining work
- Support sprint planning
- Maintain visibility of future work

## Jira Workflow

The project used a workflow that included statuses such as:

**To Do → In Progress → Done**

The Jira board provided visibility into the current status of each backlog item during sprint execution.
